# What we fixed: Sedona Office & Storage Rentals

A homepage concept by My Launch Partner. All facts come from the current sedonaofficerentals.com homepage. Nothing was invented.

**It works on phones now.** The current site has no mobile setting, so on a phone the text runs off the right edge and visitors have to pinch and scroll sideways. The new page fits any screen, and phone visitors get a "Call offices" button that stays in reach.

**One tap to call.** Both phone numbers, for offices and for Sedona Self Storage, are now tap-to-call links instead of plain text.

**The views sell themselves.** The current site's best asset, offices with red-rock views, was hidden behind a small 2008 banner. The new page opens on a full-width Sedona photo and gives the real view-from-the-office photos their own section.

**Easier to scan.** One long block of bolded keywords is now short sections: offices, views, storage and contact. Each plaza has its own link to its listing.

**Clear next steps.** "See available offices," "Explore storage" and "Call" buttons appear where people decide, instead of only in a sidebar of image buttons.

**Modern, accessible build.** The current site was built in Microsoft FrontPage 4.0. Its menu is made of pictures of words, which screen readers and Google can't read, and none of its 11 images have descriptions. The new page uses real text navigation, described images, readable type sizes and fast, compressed photos.

**Same business, refreshed look.** The color palette keeps the site's original sky blue, deepened, alongside Sedona's terracotta and sandstone, so it still feels like M & M.
