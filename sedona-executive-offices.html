A private office, sized right

Single executive offices for professionals who need a polished space without a large footprint, inside an established, well-occupied business plaza with plenty of parking. La Tierra Plaza 1120 sits on the northeast corner of SR 89A and North Airport Road.

The suite was designed as five separate offices around an inviting reception area where clients can wait comfortably. Four of the five offices have their own double doors to the outside, and windows keep the workday sunny. It's a turn-key way to relocate or start a business in one of Sedona's most sought-after areas.
