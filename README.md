# Sedona Office & Storage Rentals: concept redesign

A concept (homepage plus two inside pages) for [sedonaofficerentals.com](https://www.sedonaofficerentals.com/) (M & M Property Management Group, LLC), prepared by My Launch Partner as a pitch. This is not the official site. Every page is marked `noindex` so it never competes with the real site in search.

- `index.html`: the homepage
- `listings.html`: the office listings page
- `sedona-executive-offices.html`: a sample single-listing page (Sedona Executive Offices)
- `assets/site.css`: shared styles for all three pages
- `assets/img/`: compressed photos. The hero is licensed from Envato Elements (project "Sedona Office Rentals – concept redesign"). All other photos are the business's own, from their current site.
- `pitch/`: before/after screenshots and the plain-language "what we fixed" list

All buttons point to the business's real pages, phone numbers and email.
